I am implementing a meeting appointment slot recommender system.

The system suggests available meeting slots based on working hours, busy intervals, meeting duration, buffer time, and an optional candidate window.

Two personas influence the system requirements:

Persona 1 – Jane Adams (Primary User)

-Busy professional who prefers automation and minimal interaction
-Expects predictable and stable system behavior
-Wants the system to automatically resolve scheduling conflicts
-Dislikes excessive notifications or duplicate outputs

Persona 2 – Mo Rodriguez (Edge User)

-Requires deterministic and consistent system behavior
-Carefully reviews system outputs and expects clear handling of edge cases
-Experiences stress when system decisions appear arbitrary or unexplained
-Wants explicit handling of errors instead of silent failures

Based on these personas, what types of system constraints might be derived for a scheduling system like this?
Please focus on constraints related to reliability, determinism, error handling, and predictable system behavior.


Purpose:
To confirm that the constraints derived from the personas capture reliability, determinism, and edge case handling.

Response Summary:
The LLM suggested constraints related to deterministic ordering, conflict resolution, and explicit handling of edge cases.
